About & privacy

This is a toy. It is for fun. Nothing here is for sale and nothing here is advice.

We are not with the candy companies. The Candy Exchange is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any candy maker or brand. Brand names appear only to say which candy is which. They belong to their owners. No logos, wrappers or packaging artwork are used anywhere.

Where the prices come from. FiveThirtyEight's Ultimate Halloween Candy Power Ranking, used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. 83 candies ranked by 269,000 head-to-head matchups. We did not invent any of the numbers.

Privacy. No accounts. No names, no ages, no email addresses. No cookies. No ads. No logins. Your picks, your haul and your friends' prices live only in your own browser and are gone the moment you close the tab. They are never sent anywhere.

We do count visits. We use a privacy-friendly counter that sets no cookies and cannot follow anybody from one website to another. It tells us how many people came and roughly which country they came from, so we know whether real people are using this. That is all it tells us. There is no profile of you, nothing is tied to a person, and nothing is ever sold or shared with anyone.
